# Proposal: thumbnail-copy (editing doc v2, decision 602)

Target skill: thumbnail-copy

Why: decision 602: "titles and thumbnails as separate lists, 25 thumbnail options first, then titles to match the thumbnails Sarika picks". On Mandar's episode Sarika replaced the 12-card pair menu with 15 thumbnail lines of her own, then asked for "YT titles that are optimised for SEO based on keyword ranking has guest and company name" for those lines. Her angle list (16 Nov): "hooky, massy, contrarian, bold statement, strong idea, insightful, drives curiosity, why, what and how angles, controversial", "max 5/6 words".

The evidence base, what wins and loses, Akshay's review rules, visual direction and the factual bar stay as they are.

## Change 1: "Building the menu"
Before:
> **Ten to twelve combos.** ... Each combo carries: Thumbnail copy, Episode title, The idea, Visual concept, Alternate visual, Pattern badge, The note, Traces to, Pairs with.

After:
> **Two steps. Thumbnails first, titles second.**
> Step 1: **25 thumbnail options**, no titles. Table `# | Pick | Thumbnail copy | Angle | The idea, with timestamp | Visual concept`. Spread across angles (hooky, massy, contrarian, bold statement, strong idea, insightful, curiosity, why, what, how, controversial), at least 8 different angles, and name the angle. Screen every line against the review rules; drop failures rather than showing Avoid cards. Do not rank and do not pick; leave Pick for the editor.
> Step 2, only after the editor ticks picks: **one title per picked thumbnail**. Table `# | Picked thumbnail | YouTube title | Chars | Keyword it targets`.
