## The three behaviours

1. **Log work messages hourly.** This is the existing STEP 1 and 2, unchanged. The privacy guard stays: personal chats are skipped by name and participants, never read.
2. **Reply to "@zoya" (STEP 0z).** The message's own text has to start with "@zoya".
   - It's found by filtered reads (`query "@zoya"`), so no other personal-chat text is read. A scan Activity holds the cursor, so an outage loses nothing. Chats that aren't covered are named in the report.
   - **Akshay or a teammate:** answered from Notion, or the step is done as 0a does it. Only Akshay's own message carries his decision.
   - **Anyone else:** gets only "zoya here. it's with the team, we'll come back to you.", Mon to Fri 09:30 to 19:00 IST, and the ask is captured on the row. Nothing from Notion and nothing applied. This keeps Cleanup's 19 Oct fixes 1 and 2 in their simplest form.
   - In a personal chat, only Akshay's own mention counts.
   - Akshay asking in a chat with outsiders gets the answer in the Zoya group.
3. **Production notes to "All things FT" (STEP 2c).** While logging, each editing or production note becomes one Activity (Kind Note, Source ID "atft:<message key>").
   - The oldest of those are posted to the group, at most 10 a run, Mon to Fri 09:30 to 19:00 IST, after an exact-name group check.
   - Each post is three lines: who, what, and the work link.
   - After a confirmed send, the Activity's Kind becomes WhatsApp.
   - A note that arrives out of hours waits as its Activity. It isn't lost when the window moves.
