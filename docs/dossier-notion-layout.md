# Host dossier on Notion: the standard layout (decision 677)

Akshay, 25 Nov 2026: "make this the standard - dossiers on Notion". Every host dossier, on every show, is a Notion page under the guest's Engagement. No artifact, no Eval slot. The model page is Akshay Bector's FT dossier (https://app.notion.com/p/42319a301a7f81e581adf70d773483f3). Copy its structure and styling; the show's own dossier skill still decides the content, the voice and the questions.

Why: the first Notion dossier was "just a big block of text". This layout makes it scannable in a minute, with depth behind toggles. Write it in Notion-flavored Markdown (read notion://docs/notion-flavored-markdown-spec first). Never more than two columns side by side (Akshay, 9 Oct).

## Section order and elements

1. **Privacy line.** A gray_bg callout with a lock icon: internal or host-only, never for the guest or PR, built date and skill.
2. **Quick-scan card.** Two columns, about 58/42.
   - Left: the guest's name as an H1 with one emoji, a gray one-line role, a LinkedIn link, and a two-column table with a gray header column. The rows are recording slot, Via/PR, pre-call, who's not on the call, and set or logistics.
   - Right: three callouts, one per idea:
     - the hook (yellow_bg, ★);
     - the spine question (purple_bg, 🎯);
     - handle with care (red_bg, ⚠️).
3. **At a glance.** A full-width table with a blue_bg header row of 5 or 6 headline numbers, a bold values row, and a gray caption row giving each number's source.
4. **The business, simply.** Two sentences, then a table (green_bg header) of who buys, how, and the share of revenue. Then the core tension in an orange_bg callout.
5. **The founder, briefly.** One gray quote block, five or six sentences.
6. **Career timeline.** A toggle H3 holding a When | What table. Colour the low-point row red_bg and the milestone that makes the episode timely green_bg.
7. **The conversation, in four acts.** An H1, then one callout per act. Each act gets its own colour: Act I blue_bg, Act II orange_bg, Act III green_bg, Act IV purple_bg. Inside each act:
   - a bold act title, with a gray one-line steer;
   - for each theme, a bold title and a gray context fragment;
   - the questions as bullets, in Akshay's (or the host's) spoken voice.

   Mark the spine theme with ★.
8. **Fact flags.** A table with a red_bg header: Topic | What we found | How to handle it. The sensitive row is orange_bg.
9. **How they interview.** Two columns, two callouts: Expect (green_bg ✅) and Steer (yellow_bg 🧭).
10. **Depth behind toggles** (toggle H3, collapsed):
    - past appearances;
    - a numbers cheat sheet, as small tables with gray_bg headers: financials by year, capital events, benchmarks;
    - sources.

Use dividers (---) between the top block, the business and founder block, the acts, and the flags. Keep paragraphs short. Use colour only to mean something (a section, a warning, a low point), never for decoration.
