## Fixes ranked by impact

Each one removes work or a place, or gives a phase its own slot. None adds a new standing rule unless noted.

1. **Take capture out of the day chat's wake budget.** Make the capture composer that cleared Saturday's backlog the permanent way capture runs: one Sonnet composer per wake that writes only Activities and "Row change" notes, the same pattern SLOW already uses. The day chat then starts each wake with actions and FAST on mail that's already logged. This fixes the Thursday and Friday capture gap, the 49 to 94 hour replies and most Partial wakes. It also cuts Opus cost, since capture is the bulk of the reading.
2. **Delete the Builder queue from Engagements.** Move the live items among the 24 Builder rows to the Build board (decisions 350, 405) and Trash the rest; most reference retired routines. Remove "Builder" from Waiting on. That's one place instead of two (decision 357), and 10 of the 39 stale segments disappear.
3. **Stop re-dating blocked steps, and park them instead.** A Zoya step marked "blocked" or "held" whose token date has already passed once is no longer re-dated: the row goes to Parked with its reason, and a reply reopens it (decision 621 already does this). This applies today to the 6 un# rows with no email, the 13 B2S "approval rescinded" Decision pending rows and Chaandini's 4 paused rows. Strictly, it's one changed instruction, but it removes about 25 rows from active queues.
4. **Retire the daily sweep, or fold its live checks into SLOW.** It recorded 0 runs in 6 slots, so nothing currently depends on it. Then let SLOW run at most twice a weekday (for example 11:08 and 17:08) instead of 7 slots it can't fill. That's fewer slots and the same work done.
5. **Fix the monitor before anything else that relies on it.** Point it at the current day chat; it watches Wednesday's on Saturday. Have it alert on the zoya-day.md rule "3 Partial in a row is a failure", and drop the overnight hourly checks (about 30 of its 66 records).
6. **One field for guest-form state.** Remove the Episode "Guest form in" checkbox and read the Engagement status, or the reverse. Close the two Task rows marked Booked, and clear Mekin's stale Chaandini step.
7. **One post-recording check, with no new phase.** Fold "Editing doc and Planned release empty 3 days after the transcript lands" into the existing decision 644 per-wake transcript check (it already loops over the same Episodes). It would put one line on Sarika's Team task. This adds a rule, so it ranks last. Current exposure is 3 episodes.
8. **Arthur Hayes, today.** The dossier review handover has no date. Sri's deadline (Fri 20 Nov) has passed and the recording is Wed 25 Nov. The fix is a date on the row (data), not a rule.
