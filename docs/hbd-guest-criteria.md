# Human by Design guest bar (Arjun, October 2026)

Human by Design is FastCode AI's podcast on AI research that turns into real products, hosted by Dr. Arjun Jain (FastCode AI founder, Bengaluru; PhD at MPI Informatik Saarbruecken; postdoc at NYU with Yann LeCun and Chris Bregler; ex-IIT Bombay and IISc faculty; about 8,000 Google Scholar citations). Verify Arjun's own Google Scholar / DBLP co-author list once before you start.

Who he wants
- Rock star professors. Benchmarks: Jitendra Malik, Geoffrey Hinton, Yoshua Bengio, Andrej Karpathy, Fei-Fei Li, Bernhard Schoelkopf. The name has to carry weight on its own.
- Fortune 500 AI leaders. Reference: Philippe Rambach (Chief AI Officer, Schneider Electric).
- Founders, ideally: research that became real products. A pure academic with no company or commercial track record may not qualify (category call pending with Arjun: FLAG, don't decline, on this ground alone).

Why he turned people down (Aspuru-Guzik, Torr, Theobalt, Himanshu Joshi, Manocha, Irfan Essa, Titov, Srinath Sridhar, Edward A. Lee): boring work; nothing notable recently ("walking towards sunset"); fewer citations than Arjun (~8,000 is the floor); worked with Arjun before ("small fry").

Hard rules
- Quantum-focused: DECLINE.
- Worked with Arjun (co-authored, same lab, supervised him or was supervised by him, colleague in the same group): DECLINE.
- Merely knows Arjun: FLAG (his call).
- India-based: FLAG, don't decline (non-India preferred).
- Europe-based: not a hard no. Check everything else; present if they clear it.
- Anti-ICP: defense or classified work, regulated healthcare with patient-data residency, simulation-only work: DECLINE.
- Citations under ~8,000 (researchers only; not applied to Fortune 500 executives): DECLINE.
- Nothing notable in the last ~3 years ("walking towards sunset"): DECLINE.

Final filter: would this person hold their own in a list next to Fei-Fei Li or Karpathy? If the answer needs explaining, he'll probably pass (DECLINE or FLAG).

Fortune 500 AI leaders: test against Philippe Rambach: the top AI executive (Chief AI Officer, Chief AI/Data Officer, head of AI) at a Fortune Global 500 / Fortune 500 company, with a visible public profile (talks, press). A mid-level director, a non-Fortune-500 company, or no public footprint: DECLINE. Regulated healthcare with patient-data residency as the core AI work: DECLINE (anti-ICP).

Verdicts
- PRESENT: clears every rule and the final filter.
- FLAG: Arjun's call (India-based, merely knows Arjun, pure academic with no company but otherwise a rock star, or genuinely borderline).
- DECLINE: any hard rule fails, or clearly below the bar.
