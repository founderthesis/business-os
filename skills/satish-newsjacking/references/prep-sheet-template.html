Zepto's IPO papers, and the ownership story inside them

Zepto filed an updated DRHP on 8 June. Beyond the headline IPO size, the filing opens up four threads that sit squarely on your beat: a pre-IPO secondary, a founder cap table held mostly through trusts, large executive option grants, and an ED summons. Facts below are verified across Business Standard, Entrackr and ANI. Your view leads, this is only the ground.

Filed: 8 Jun 2026 (public 9 Jun) IPO: ₹8,010 Cr fresh + OFS Status: Fresh — not previously posted
What the filing shows
01

A ₹577 Cr secondary, months before the IPO

₹577 Cr total Nexus VII ₹356 Cr Kaiser ₹190 Cr Razor ₹31 Cr

Motilal Oswal Financial Services bought roughly ₹577 crore of shares from existing investors in a secondary completed on 22 August 2025. Nexus Venture VII was the largest seller at about ₹356 crore, Kaiser Permanente Group Trust nearly ₹190 crore, and Razor Ventures around ₹31 crore.

Context worth holding: earlier in 2025, Zepto was already in talks for a ~$250 million secondary with the PE arms of Motilal Oswal and Edelweiss, framed openly as a way to lift Indian ownership before listing. This is a deliberate pre-IPO liquidity pattern, not a one-off.

02

Who actually owns Zepto

Promoter group 19.56% Palicha 1.07% Vohra 0.89% ESOP Trust 7.46%

On a fully diluted basis the promoter group holds 19.56%, but the two founders directly own only about 1.07% and 0.89%. The bulk sits in family trusts, Lazarus Trust at 9.03% and The Vohra Trust at 7.48%.

Among outside investors, Nexus leads at 13.12% combined, then Glade Brook 7.73%, the Zepto ESOP Trust 7.46%, StepStone 7.34%, LGF Scale's Mars funds 5.58%, and Y Combinator entities 5.03%.

03

Large option grants to the senior bench

CFO Bafna 13.83 Cr CBO Meel 5.19 Cr CHRO Arora 2.13 Cr Total pool 1.21 Bn

CFO Ramesh Bafna was allotted 13.83 crore options, one of the single largest grants in the plan. CBO Devendra Meel got 5.19 crore, CHRO Sneha Arora 2.13 crore, CPO Ankit Agrawal 1.32 crore, and CGO Divesh Sawhney 87.8 lakh.

Across the company there are more than 1.21 billion outstanding options, which makes the ESOP Trust one of Zepto's largest single shareholders.

Handle with care
04

An ED summons under FEMA

The filing discloses that founders Aadit Palicha and Kaivalya Vohra received Enforcement Directorate summonses in April 2026 under FEMA. The agency sought information on foreign investments, overseas entities, shareholding structure, loans, guarantees and bank accounts.

Both founders appeared and submitted the requested documents. The prospectus records no finding of wrongdoing against the founders or the company. The safe framing is exactly that: summoned, complied, nothing alleged. Anything that reads as implying guilt is a real risk on a named, listing-bound company.

What to weigh before you write

The non-obvious story the herd is missing: who owns this company, and who gets liquidity, and when.

Most coverage is reading the secondary and the grants as a celebration, growth doubling, investors cashing in, the young-founder arc. The quieter read is the one only an operator makes. Founders holding around 2% directly with the rest in trusts. A CFO sitting on 13.83 crore options. A secondary that handed early investors cash well before the public ever got a look. That is a cap table and liquidity-sequencing story, which is your territory, not the growth-numbers story everyone else will run.

Angle territory (your call): when a secondary clusters right before an IPO, is it reward for the journey, or the cap table getting tidied and de-risked ahead of public scrutiny? You can raise the question honestly without answering it for the reader.
Two factual flags. The ED line must stay on the "complied, no wrongdoing alleged" side. And the grant and ownership figures here come from the filing as reported by Business Standard and Entrackr, not yet read off the SEBI document itself, so confirm any number that lands on a thumbnail or in the post body.
