# Founder Thesis — LinkedIn posts

Four variants, three voices, one episode. In the copy doc they fill tabs 3 to 6
(decision 527). Produce whichever is asked for; produce variants 2 to 4 if the
ask is open. Variant 1 is written only when Akshay asks for a specific post.

## Inputs

- **Transcript** (required)
- **Research dossier** (optional but preferred)
- **Selected theme** (optional) from `ft-episode-themes`
- **Episode link** — use `[episode link]` as a placeholder if not supplied

## Shared craft rules

- **Open on a thesis or an anti-thesis**, one or two sentences, that makes someone stop scrolling. Not a summary, not "In this episode".
- **Then build** with specifics from the transcript and the dossier. Named companies, real numbers, the actual mechanism.
- **Paragraphs of two sentences maximum.** LinkedIn is read in a narrow column at speed.
- **Goal is curiosity, click-through and comments.** Not SEO.
- **Riches in niches.** The reader is an urban, ambitious, Shark Tank-watching professional. Where it helps, explain the business plainly and compare it with an Indian brand or model they know.
- **No hyperbole.** Banned: "here's what blew my mind", "here's what shocked me", "revolution", "game-changing", "disrupting". Professional register throughout.
- **No em dashes.**

## The four variants

### 1. Akshay's thought-leadership posts (on request only)

The copy doc's "AD thought leadership" tab starts with a blank "Your posts"
section for Ranjita and a list of post ideas. Write a full post only when Akshay
or Ranjita asks for one; it goes below the ideas as a draft.

Written in Akshay's voice: direct, curious, unimpressed by hype, comfortable with a specific number. He is the person who spent an hour asking, so the post can carry what he found surprising and where he pushed back.

CTA shape: *"I unpack all of this with [Founder], [Role] of [Company], on the Founder Thesis Podcast: [episode link]"*

### 2. Akshay's release post (3 options)

Short posts for Akshay's account to go out with the video trailer. Each sentence is its own paragraph, three sentences at most. Number them Option 1, 2 and 3, each on a different angle.

- Built for click-through with a curiosity hook
- Pitched at builders and startup enthusiasts
- Must name the guest, the company and the podcast inside the sentences, not as a tagline

### 3. Guest's own account (two types)

Written as the guest, in the guest's voice. Read their recent LinkedIn posts where you can and match how they write now; otherwise match how they talk in the transcript. These are handed over for them to publish, so never put words in their mouth they would not use.

**Type 1: repost captions for Akshay's release post.** Four captions, each 2 to 4 short lines:
- **A, B and C** each pair with Akshay's release post Options 1, 2 and 3, on the same angle.
- **D** works with any of his posts.
- Keep them general: they sit on top of Akshay's post, so never answer or quote a specific line in it ("here's my answer"). The guest may repost a different option from the one you expect.
- Open with an instruction line for the guest: when Akshay's post goes live, click Repost, choose "Repost with your thoughts" and paste the caption that pairs with it; if unsure, use D.

**Type 2: a standalone post with the trailer attached.** One recommended post, written to drive views: a hook in the guest's own register, what the conversation covers, and the CTA. Open with an instruction line: post from your own account and attach the trailer video we share. Add up to three alternates on other angles after it.

The guest can say things the host cannot: what they got wrong, what they would do differently, what the industry misunderstands.

CTA shape: *"I unpack all of this with Akshay Datt on the Founder Thesis Podcast: [episode link]"*

### 4. Guest's company page (two types)

Corporate voice, third person about the founder. The company page speaks about the business and the category, not about personal feelings.

- **Type 1: repost captions A to D**, paired with Akshay's release post options exactly as in variant 3, with an instruction line for the page admin.
- **Type 2: a standalone announcement** of the founder's appearance on the podcast, with the trailer attached and an instruction line for the page admin. Up to three alternates about the business after it.

CTA shape: *"[Guest name] unpacks all of this with Akshay Datt on the Founder Thesis Podcast: [episode link]"*, or "Watch the full episode: [episode link]" for the announcement.

## What the channel data suggests

From the FT Channel Rules page (https://app.notion.com/p/3d219a301a7f813fa919fe4b6c48a72f: read it, it supersedes this):

- **Claim, not category.** The split that governs thumbnails governs hooks too. "The Climate AI Opportunity" is a topic label; "Fixing supply beats creating demand" is a claim someone can agree or disagree with. Open on the claim.
- **Borrowed referents travel.** Anchoring an unknown company against something the reader already knows is the strongest observed pattern on the channel.
- **Investor framings underperform.** If the guest is an investor, lead with the operating story, not the fund.

## Hard rules

- **Every number appears in the transcript or the dossier.** Never estimate. Tracxn figures may be used, cited as "Source: Tracxn" (decision 532). Posts can link to any source.
- **Never put a claim in the guest's mouth they did not make.** These posts go out under their name.
- **Accurate but generous.** These are people FT hosted.
- Output ready-to-publish copy, not drafts with brackets, except for the episode link placeholder.
- **Formatting in the copy doc:** headings bold and coloured (teal for sections and recommended picks, purple for alternates), a horizontal rule between posts, the recommended option marked ★.
