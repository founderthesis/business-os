# Founder Thesis Host Dossier — Section-by-Section Spec

Read before drafting (Stage 3). Then read `akshay-question-voice.md` before writing any question.

One HTML file, skimmable in ~5 minutes on a phone. **Dark mode**: dark background (`#12151a` or similar), light text, one accent colour, a second accent for flags. A sticky in-page nav bar at the top links to every section by anchor id, so Akshay can jump straight to Themes without scrolling.

Filename: `/mnt/user-data/outputs/<lastname>_ft_dossier.html`

Section order (fixed): **Header → Business, simply → Founder, briefly → Career timeline → Themes (four acts) → Fact flags → Past appearances → Snapshot → Appendix (Numbers cheat sheet, Sources)**. Snapshot and the numbers cheat sheet are reference material, not front matter — they go after the parts Akshay actually reads first.

---

## Header block

Kicker (`FOUNDER THESIS · HOST DOSSIER`), guest name as H1, one-line role and pedigree. **A LinkedIn profile link as a visible button/pill directly under the role line — not buried in the meta block.** Then a small meta block: recording date/time, who referred the guest, PR contact, pre-call date, and any co-founder who is NOT on the recording.

## Sticky nav

A thin sticky bar pinned to the top of the page, below the header, with anchor links to every major section (Business, Founder, Timeline, Themes, Fact flags, Past appearances, Snapshot, Appendix). Small, unobtrusive, dark-mode styled.

## § The business, simply

**One condensed section, not five.** A short paragraph (roughly 80-120 words) explaining where this business sits in the stack of its market: what it does, what sits upstream of it, what sits downstream, and which layer it actually occupies. Written for a smart 20-year-old, no jargon left unexplained.

Follow the paragraph with a **simple visual stack diagram** — three or four boxes in a row (upstream → the company's layer, highlighted → downstream), each with a one-line label. This replaces the old five-part money-flow/margin-pools/timeline breakdown; that level of detail was too much to read before recording and now lives, where it's genuinely load-bearing, inside individual themes or the appendix.

One closing italic line is enough for the non-obvious implication or the tension the business model creates. Do not rebuild the old money-flow table or the market-pools bullet list here — cut, don't relocate.

## § The founder, briefly

One paragraph, 100-150 words. Not school → jobs → founding — the arc and the stakes: what they bet, what broke, what they're proving now. This is the prose version; the career timeline section right after it carries the dates and the detail.

## § Career timeline

A **visual vertical timeline**, not a paragraph. Each entry: a year/period label, a short title, one line of description. Personal milestones and company milestones are folded into the same timeline (same visual stream), but company entries get a distinct marker colour/tag so the two threads stay readable at a glance.

Include, where relevant: origin/early-life detail that explains the founder's motivation, education, prior non-outcomes (tagged as such, stated plainly), each venture's founding date, exit or transition dates, and — importantly — what happened to a past company **after** the founder left, if that history is part of the tension (e.g. a company's later raise, decline, or trouble, shown as its own timeline entries even though the founder wasn't there for them). This is what lets the timeline set up Act II without extra prose.

## § Themes, in four acts

Unchanged from before. Each act gets a label and one italic line of direction for Akshay.

**Act I · Grounding** (2-3 themes). Category decode, the 101, who pays whom.

**Act II · The story, with a low point** (2-3 themes). The leap, and the hardest stretch. Hunt the low point deliberately. If the public record has none, the gap IS the lead.

**Act III · Mechanics** (3-4 themes). Model, unit economics, growth engine, tech, regulation.

**Act IV · Hard questions** (2 themes). Genuine puzzles the guest can argue. Mark the sharpest `★ SPINE`, styled as a highlighted block. Mark anything from the last few days `⚡ BREAKING`.

**Per theme:** catchy specific heading, one context line carrying the fact that powers it, 2-4 questions in Akshay's register (`akshay-question-voice.md`). Target ≤75 words for the theme.

No index box needed at the top of this section — the sticky nav already gives the at-a-glance jump. Act labels plus theme headings, read top to bottom, are the arc.

## § Fact flags

2-6 flags, each in a tinted box with a short uppercase label. Framed as **housekeeping, not prosecution**.

## § Past appearances, and how he interviews

Unchanged. State first whether they've been on Founder Thesis before (check the master guest sheet). Bulleted list of every prior podcast/interview, **each hyperlinked inline**. Close with **the read**.

## § Snapshot

Moved here, after Themes and Fact flags and Past appearances — it's the pre-record glance, not the opening pitch. Keep the stat strip (5-6 big numbers) directly above the snapshot table, both in this section now rather than at the top of the page.

9-12 rows in the table:

| Field | Content rule |
|---|---|
| Company | Name + one-phrase descriptor, plus any recent rebrand |
| Entities | Legal entities with incorporation dates and which one holds the licence or IP |
| Timeline | Incorporation, launch, first revenue, major product milestones, in one line |
| HQ | City, area |
| Revenue | Latest FY figure + FY label + source grade. Say plainly if not yet public |
| Funding | Total raised, round sequence, leads |
| Valuation | **Peak (date) AND current (date) if they differ** |
| Investors | 2-4 recognizable names |
| Scale | The operating metric that matters in this category |
| Headcount | Number + source; note conflicts |
| The hook | One line: why this conversation, now |

## § Appendix

Two collapsible (`<details>`) blocks at the very bottom, closed by default:

**Numbers cheat sheet** — the VC-analyst layer, for Akshay to open only if he wants it. Same three blocks as before: funding table (round, date, amount, lead, valuation), operating bullets (every number with year + source grade), benchmark table (company vs 2-3 named competitors), closing industry-context paragraph.

**Sources** — grouped: Company and funding / Industry / Quotes and founder material. Every figure and quote traces to a clickable link here, graded (filing-grade, press, company-reported, wire release, directional).

---

## What is deliberately NOT in this dossier

- No five-part industry breakdown up top — one paragraph plus a stack diagram, full detail (if any) lives in the appendix or inside themes.
- No chronological prose journey — the timeline section carries the dates, the founder paragraph carries the arc.
- No "why this question lands" coaching notes under questions.
- No separate one-pager — the stat strip and Snapshot, now positioned after Themes, are the reference layer.
- No production-meta language ("Stage 2", "Phase", "Working Draft").
- No verified/unverified badges on individual claims outside the flag block.
- No light/white theme. Dark mode only.
