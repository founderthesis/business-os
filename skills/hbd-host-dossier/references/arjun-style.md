# Host calibration: Dr. Arjun Jain

## Who he is, only as it affects questions
PhD Max Planck, postdoc under Yann LeCun at NYU, autonomous driving at Apple, computer vision IP sold to Mercedes-Benz and shipping in production cars, now founder of FastCode AI. He has debugged models that had to survive contact with a factory floor. Write questions that assume that, and never write one that explains a basic concept back to him.

## Register
- **Conversational and accessible, unscripted.** He can go deep, but pulls back so a non-PhD listener stays with him.
- **Short and blunt when it matters.** "Be honest." "What broke?" "Is that a real product or a rebrand?"
- **Anchored to a named number, system, vendor, year or customer.** "Is that a Schneider product or a Microsoft product with Schneider branding?" is a question. "How do you think about partnerships?" is a category.
- **Excited by what people build and at what scale.** Not by awards, citations, h-indices or rankings. Keep those out entirely.
- **Asks for the worked example.** What did one customer actually come with, and what did the thing look like for them.

## Question construction rules
- One sentence. Compound questions get the last clause answered and nothing else.
- Every question must be answerable with a story, not a position.
- Prefer cost, trade-off and counterfactual over achievement. "What did it cost?" beats "What did you achieve?" every time.
- Name the thing. A question with no proper noun in it is a category, and categories get the rehearsed answer.

## Banned moves
- Credential theatre of any kind.
- Softballs at Dial 3, kernel internals at Dial 1.
- Anything answerable from the company homepage.
- Questions that presuppose a fact the pack has flagged as Tier B or unverified.

## What to do when he is handed a strong guest
Many guests are more media-trained than he is. His advantage is that he has built the thing under discussion. The pack should always give him at least one place where his own production experience lets him ask something a journalist structurally cannot.
