# The body of work

The single source of truth for what Unnati has already built. Read at Phase 5 and
use it for the Why us section, which since October 2026 is the only credibility
placement in a pitch (see `editorial-rules.md` under "Credibility"). Update this file when a number moves, the same way
`pricing-defaults.md` gets updated when a rate moves.

**Every name below was checked against a published episode page on 24 September
2026.** Anything that could not be confirmed sits in "Gaps to fill" at the end rather
than in a table. Do not promote a name out of that section without a link.

**Numbers discipline.** Every figure here is verified and none of them get rounded up
in a document. This is a credibility business selling a credibility product, and a
pitch that inflates its own numbers has lost the argument it is making. If a figure is
stale or you cannot source it, leave it out rather than estimating it. An honest gap
costs nothing; one invented subscriber count costs the whole section.

**Do not put a "we never invent numbers" claim in a client document.** It was drafted
and cut. Saying it out loud invites the reader to go looking, and a document that
protests its own honesty sounds like one that needs to. The discipline shows in the
figures being checkable, not in a sentence about the discipline.

## The show links

Every show name in a pitch is a hyperlink to its YouTube channel. A reader who wants
to check goes one click, and the section stops being a claim.

| Show | YouTube |
|---|---|

| Built to Share | https://www.youtube.com/@built2share |
| un# (Unhashed) | https://www.youtube.com/@unhashedpodcast |
| Human by Design | https://www.youtube.com/@HumanByDesignPodcast |

All four confirmed live. Use only these handles; other spellings of the un# channel return 404.

Link the show name itself, not a "watch here" phrase beside it. In the Word build the
show name carries the hyperlink the same way, so the two deliverables match. Where a
document cannot carry links, the section still reads correctly without them: never
write a sentence that depends on the reader clicking.

Built to Share also publishes on built2share.hissa.com and Apple Podcasts, and un#
uses short links of the form unhashed.co/[guest]. Spotify and Apple links exist for
all four. None of them go in a pitch: one link per show, and YouTube is the one a
prospect will actually open.

## All four shows, every time

**The range is the argument.** Four unrelated arenas, four hosts who are not us, and
a method that worked in all of them. Three shows looks like a portfolio, four looks
like a practice.

un# is the one that gets dropped, because crypto feels off-topic next to a lending
client or a university. Keep it. It carries the biggest numbers of the four, and a
prospect who sees a show about DeFi working for a DeFi founder draws the obvious
conclusion about their own field.

**Founder Thesis goes first**, because it follows the origin paragraph and finishes
the thought. Order the other three by closeness to this prospect's world, nearest
first. Pick guest names the same way: the Indian founder names for an Indian B2B
prospect, the institutional names for a financial one, the DeFi names for crypto.

Per show, the pitch carries the host, the arena, what it did for that host's business,
and two to four guest names a reader will recognise. The outcome line matters more
than the numbers: a prospect is buying a result, not a subscriber count.

## Spellings that get got wrong

**Collisions inside one document.** These are the likely errors.

- **Visham Sikand** (1Buy.ai) and **Vishal Bali** (Asia Healthcare Holdings) are two
  different Built to Share guests.
- Two **Ravi**: Ravi Saxena (Wonderchef), Ravi Bhushan (BrightCHAMPS).
- Three **Jain**: Anurag Jain (CarDekho, Built to Share), Ujjwal Jain (WealthDesk),
  Amit Jain (CarDekho, Founder Thesis, and Anurag's brother).
- **Ankit Mehta** is one person on two shows. Never list him in both sections.

**Individual spellings.**

- **Sanjeev Bikhchandani.** The k before the h is the one people drop.
- **Jürgen Schmidhuber.** **Schneider Electric.** **ideaForge**, lowercase i, capital
  F. **IndiaMART.** **CarDekho.** **Rajendra S. Pawar.** **TeamLease.**
- **Sid Powell**, never "Sidney Powell", which is also a US attorney of
  election-litigation notoriety. Write "Sid Powell, CEO, Maple Finance".
- **Yifan He** is Chinese name order. Surname He, given name Yifan. Second reference
  is "Mr. He".
- **Sébastien Borget** carries the accent even though the show drops it.
- **Mriganka Pattnaik**, double t. **Jakob Palmstierna**, k and -stierna. **Matthew
  Hougan**, not Hogan, and often published as Matt. **Marc Boiron**, c not k. **Nikhil
  Chandhok**, with the d-h. **Atul Shinghal**, not Singhal. **Pravin Agarwala**, not
  Agarwal. **Ujjwal Jain**, double l. **Madhusudan Ekambaram.** **Harshvardhan
  Lunia.** **Ajeya Motaganahalli.** **Offchain Labs**, full name.
- Hosts: **Satish Mugulavalli** and **Sri Misra**, not Mishra. He appears as both Sri
  and Srikumar.

Getting a guest's name wrong in the section that proves you book senior guests is the
most expensive typo in the document.
