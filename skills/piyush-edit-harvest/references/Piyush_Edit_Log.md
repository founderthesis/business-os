# Piyush Edit Log

Running record of changes Piyush makes to drafts, harvested by `piyush-edit-harvest`.
One line per substantive change. Used to separate one-off edits from recurring patterns,
and to decide when a pattern gets promoted to a standing skill rule.

Promotion thresholds:
- Fact fixes (bucket 1): promote on first sight. Facts can't be wrong.
- Voice / structure / compression / India (buckets 3-6): flag at 2, promote at 3.
- Research gaps (bucket 2): raise the dossier scorecard at 2.

Format:
`[date] | [post / brand] | [bucket] | [before → after] | [implied rule] | [count]`

Buckets: 1 fact-fix · 2 research-gap · 3 voice · 4 structure · 5 compression · 6 india-turn
