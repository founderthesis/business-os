# Calendar sync

Scheduled routine "Calendar sync", every hour at :35 JST (AR-20261019-6, 20 Oct; was three times a day, decisions 174, 231), with the Google Calendar, Notion, Gmail and Google Drive connectors.

Three jobs, in order:
1. **Calendar to Notion.** Recording and precall events on the ad@thepodium.in calendar set Next date, Call type and Call link on the Engagement, move it to Booked for a recording, and hands cancellations to Zoya on Founder Thesis (Waiting on Zoya, decision 223) or the show's coordinator elsewhere. It is the only routine that sets Next date from the calendar (decision 81).
2. **Notion to calendar.** A private prep entry on Akshay's calendar for every upcoming recording and precall, with the Zencastr, Riverside or Meet link in it plus the eval, dossier, LinkedIn and Engagement links. No guests, no notifications. Recordings get a 30-minute block before the call; precalls get an entry at the same time as the call, marked free, so no extra time is blocked.
3. **After a recording (decision 173).** Zencastr audio in Drive proves the recording happened and sets Recorded; with no audio, a note goes on the row, not on Akshay's list (decision 321). Once Recorded, the thank-you is drafted in Akshay's Gmail for him to send with the transcript (decision 300, until Zencastr transcripts are automated), the WhatsApp blurb is written, and Akshay gets "invite to the FT Community" unless the guest is already in. A precall where Akshay invited the guest (read from the Gemini notes) puts "WhatsApp addition pending" on his list.

4. **Calendar audit (decision 209).** Every run checks the next 30 days against the Calendar rules page (Notion 3fa19a301a7f816c9a53fe80557d0046): double bookings (Zoya asks whoever booked), clashes, out of office over a call, missing studio links (added to the invite when the link exists), prep entries that don't match, and no room for prep or cool-off. Conflicts and possible missed meetings are noted on the row, never put on Akshay's list: he pings Zoya on WhatsApp when he wants one handled (decision 321). Anyone who books time with Akshay gets a Person and an Engagement; anything that isn't a show guest goes on the show "AD Personal". After a precall, new actions from the notes go on Akshay's list.

Never creates, moves or edits an event that has guests on it, except its own host "Podcast prep" invite (decision 129) and adding the studio link to a recording invite (decision 209). Decisions 66 to 68.
