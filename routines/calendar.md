# Calendar sync

Scheduled routine "Calendar sync", three times a day about 35 minutes after each inbox run (07:35, 15:35, 21:35 JST), with the Google Calendar, Notion, Gmail and Google Drive connectors.

Three jobs, in order:
1. **Calendar to Notion.** Recording and precall events on the ad@thepodium.in calendar set Next date, Call type and Call link on the Engagement, move it to Booked for a recording, and put cancellations on Mamatha (Waiting on). It is the only routine that sets Next date from the calendar (decision 81).
2. **Notion to calendar.** A private prep entry on Akshay's calendar for every upcoming recording and precall, with the Zencastr, Riverside or Meet link in it plus the eval, dossier, LinkedIn and Engagement links. No guests, no notifications. Recordings get a 30-minute block before the call; precalls get an entry at the same time as the call, marked free, so no extra time is blocked.
3. **After a recording (decision 173).** Zencastr audio in Drive proves the recording happened and sets Recorded; with no audio, Akshay is asked. Once Recorded, the thank-you goes out on its own with the transcript shared from Drive, the WhatsApp blurb is written, and Akshay gets "invite to the FT Community" unless the guest is already in. A precall where Akshay invited the guest (read from the Gemini notes) puts "WhatsApp addition pending" on his list.

Never creates, moves or edits an event that has guests on it, except its own host "Podcast prep" invite (decision 129). Decisions 66 to 68.
