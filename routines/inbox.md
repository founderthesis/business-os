Scheduled routine "Inbox to Business OS", four times a day at 00:00, 08:00, 16:00 and 21:00 JST, every day (decisions 202, 231; was 09:00, 16:00, 22:00, decision 174), on Sonnet (decision 138, Akshay, 12 Oct; was once a night), with the Gmail, Notion and Google Calendar connectors. New Founder Thesis pitches get their eval from the Cowork evals task 25 minutes later. Host boards have their own routine (`routines/host-boards.md`, decision 261).

Capture first. It reads the ad@thepodium.in mailbox and writes down what happened: one Activity per email, and the Engagements and Deals moved to match. It does three things beyond capture, all approved by Akshay on 5 Oct:
- New FT and un# guest pitch: replies "thanks <first name>, adding @Zoya to coordinate" (decisions 195, 199).
- Calendar RSVP mails (Accepted, Declined, Maybe): archived (never marked read, decision 199).
- Guest form submissions (the Notion guest form): merged into the guest's Engagement, People and Episode, and, on the shows Mamatha runs, the row flagged for her to send the release date.
- Waiting on: anything that needs a person gets Waiting on (who) and a Next action (what). Next date is set only for new leads; the calendar routine sets call dates (decision 81). Every Engagement it logs mail on gets Last activity.

Evals run in the Cowork task (`routines/cowork-evals.md`), calendar work in another (`routines/calendar.md`).
