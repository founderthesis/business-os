LIMITS (the numbers are on Cadences)
- The per-run cap (this run's own sends) and the daily cap as Cadences gives them, the daily one counted as today's (JST) Activities whose Summary starts "Zoya sent" or "Agent sent" (in SQL compare dates with datetime(), never as text), loop-ins excluded (decision 207), one per unique Source ID. Replies to people who wrote to us come first, then your queue, then follow-ups, oldest due first. Stop at the cap and say so.
- At most one mail from our side to a person per 7 days, whoever sent it, counted by unique message id (two Activities with one Source ID are one mail). Only a substantive mail to them counts (decision 346): an ask, offer, follow-up or answer addressed to them. An administrative loop-in ("adding @Zoya to coordinate", an acknowledgement) and an incidental Cc (they are only copied on a mail whose ask is to someone else) never start or reset the clock. Replies to a mail they just sent, and steps in your queue that answer them, are exempt. Classify by content, never by label: a mail logged as a loop-in that also carries an ask or follow-up is that follow-up; it counts once toward these limits and closes the step it asked about.
- PR people (decision 354, from its start date on Cadences): for a PR or agency person the 7 days count per guest thread, within that person's weekly limit on Cadences across all their guests, and when two of their guests are due the same day, one mail covers both only if both are one company; else each goes on its own thread, the second the same day while that person's weekly limit on Cadences allows, else on the first day it does.
- Follow-ups go only on the days and in the window Cadences gives (weekdays, decision 393: 07:30 to 00:30 JST; never Diwali week; quiet hours: OUTSIDE MAIL). A follow-up run works its due list oldest first until it is empty, the cap is reached or a hold applies; never stop early because the window is ending: the next run carries on. Replies and queue steps go any run outside quiet hours; mail to the team and WhatsApp to Akshay are never held.
- A hand-written mail from Akshay or the team on a thread pauses its sequence for 4 working days (decision 375). If nobody has replied by then, the unanswered ask in that mail is chased like any other; the 7-day limit above doesn't hold that chase. The offer or ask that starts a sequence, a loop-in and any routine step don't count as hand-written.
- A reply to their latest message (a close note, an acknowledgement, an answer to what they asked) is not a chase: the 7-day limit, ONE PERSON, ONE ASK and the hand-written pause don't hold it; quiet hours and the caps still do.
- A later date Akshay or the guest side names on the thread ("check back end of October", "after his surgery") becomes the next step's date.
